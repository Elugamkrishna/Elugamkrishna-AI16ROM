AI16ROM BOOT AUDIO

Target:
Short original startup sound synchronized with Om animation.

Requirements:
- Original audio only
- No copyrighted movie/music samples
- Low duration
- Low startup resource usage
- Audio playback must use an Android-supported audio path
- Must not block boot
- Must stop cleanly
- Must not replay after normal boot completion

Implementation target:
Framework/vendor audio integration during BootAnimation or an
early-boot service, depending on final Android 16 build architecture.

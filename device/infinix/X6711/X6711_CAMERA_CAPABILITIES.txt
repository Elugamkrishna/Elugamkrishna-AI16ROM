X6711 STOCK CAMERA BASELINE
===========================

SoC:
MediaTek MT6833 / MT6833GP

Stock camera stack:
- MediaTek Camera HAL
- Transsion Camera HAL
- CamKit
- camerahalserver
- vendor.transsion.hardware.trancam.trancamserver@1.0-service

Observed vendor components:
- Camera Provider
- ATMS
- BGService
- ISPHAL
- CCAP
- LomoEffect
- Transsion Trancam

Important:
Camera filenames/mode libraries alone do not prove that every
resolution, FPS, stabilization or Pro control is exposed by the
X6711 Camera2 HAL.

AI16ROM will expose camera features only after HAL capability
validation on actual X6711 hardware.
